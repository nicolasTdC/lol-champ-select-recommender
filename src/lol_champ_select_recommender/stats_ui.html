Thresholds

Picks: Soft requires 20+ games and 52%+ win rate overall. Hard also requires that in the selected lane. Extrapolated includes fewer than 20 games with fewer than 9.6 losses. Whitelisted applies the champion blacklist.

Lanes: Hard requires 20+ games and 52%+ WR. Soft also accepts qualifying low-sample lanes. Not Recommended is the exact complement, including insufficient samples.

Bans: your WR against enemy champions below 52% with 20+ encounters, or 10+ losses in fewer than 20 encounters for extrapolated lists. Lane-specific bans refer to the enemy lane. Pick blacklists do not filter bans.
